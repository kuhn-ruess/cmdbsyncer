{# Compact layout for the custom fields of an account. A plugin seeds up to 20 of them. As the stock inline cards — a heading, a label above every input — they made the form scroll for screens. Here every field is one row: drag handle, name, value, remove button. The labels move into the placeholders. With `collapse_unset` (the edit form) the fields without a value are folded away behind a toggle. The create form keeps them open, there they are what is still to be filled in. #}
